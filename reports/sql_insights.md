# SQL Analysis Insights

## Main KPIs

- Total tickets: 100,000
- Within SLA: 46,530
- Breached tickets: 13,583
- Not resolved tickets: 39,887
- SLA breach rate: 13.58%
- Average resolution time: 45.01 hours
- Average CSAT score: 2.24 / 5
- Reopen rate: 5.05%

## Monthly Ticket Trend

The dataset covers January 2022 to December 2025. Ticket volume remains mostly stable at around 2,000-2,200 tickets per month. The highest ticket volume appears in July 2023 with 2,263 tickets, while the lowest appears in February 2022 with 1,835 tickets.

## Product Area Analysis

Product-area workload is evenly distributed. Each product area contributes around 14% of total tickets, with Mobile App slightly highest at 14.43% and Notifications lowest at 14.10%.

## Channel Analysis

Ticket volume is evenly distributed across all support channels. Phone Transcript has the highest share at 20.13%, followed by Email at 20.09%. Chat has the lowest share at 19.83%.

## Resolution Time by Priority

Average resolution time decreases as ticket urgency increases. Low-priority tickets take the longest to resolve at 55.61 hours, while urgent tickets are resolved fastest at 26.71 hours.

## Customer Satisfaction Analysis

Customer satisfaction strongly follows sentiment. Very negative tickets have the lowest average CSAT score at 1.05, while very positive tickets have the highest score at 3.51. Neutral tickets form the largest group with 32,634 tickets and an average CSAT of 2.46.

Average CSAT is almost equal across all regions, ranging from 2.23 to 2.25. This suggests that low satisfaction is a global issue rather than a region-specific issue.

## Reopen Analysis

Out of 100,000 tickets, 5,045 tickets were reopened, giving an overall reopen rate of 5.05%. Phone Transcript has the highest reopen rate at 5.18%, while Web Form has the lowest reopen rate at 4.83%.

## SLA Breach Analysis

Mobile App has the highest SLA breach rate at 14.28%, followed by API Integration at 13.91% and Login/Auth at 13.55%. Billing has the lowest breach rate at 13.13%.

Account Access has the highest issue-type SLA breach rate at 13.87%, followed by Feature Request at 13.77%, How-To at 13.75%, and Billing Problem at 13.72%.

## High-Risk Queue

The high-risk queue contains unresolved urgent and high-priority tickets. These tickets should be reviewed first because they are most likely to create SLA risk and poor customer experience.
